Hexhaven Island

The living board from the Hexhaven party game. Every tile is drawn and animated in real time, and the wind, the sea and the time of day all change as you watch.

Morning

What lives on each tile

Forest · wood

Pines and oaks bend with each gust. Deer cross between clearings to graze, and flocks burst out of the canopy.

Pasture · wool

Sheep stand in the meadow, lowering their heads to graze and lifting them to look around. Grass sways and a butterfly drifts past.

Fields · grain

The wheat bends as gusts roll across the island. The windmill speeds up in the wind and crows circle the scarecrow.

Hills · brick

Kiln smoke trails off with the wind and the fire flickers. A worker wheels clay from the pit to the brick stacks.

Quarry · ore

Blue ore veins glint, clouds drift past the peaks and pebbles tumble down. An eagle circles while the mine cart rolls out with a load.

Desert · bandit's hideout

Heat shimmers over the dunes, sand blows in the wind, a tumbleweed bounces through and a camel caravan plods past.

Around the island, sailing boats leave wakes, boats bob at every harbour, gulls wheel overhead and fish leap. At night the windows light up and the moon rises over the water. If your device asks for reduced motion, everything shows as a still picture.
